# Code of conduct

switchyard is a small project. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is
expected to be respectful and to keep the discussion about the work.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to Claude Code.
- Disagree with the idea, not the person. Give reasons and accept that others have different needs.
- Say what you tried and what happened when you report a problem, and say what you would like when you
  ask for a feature.
- Do not post credentials, tokens or private data, yours or anyone else's.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or repeated demands for work or answers.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies wherever the project is discussed in its name: the issue tracker, pull requests and the
repository's other spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that do not follow this, and
may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, use a private report on the repository's Security tab (it reaches only the maintainer),
or contact [@LuGG000](https://github.com/LuGG000) on GitHub. Reports are treated confidentially.
